Add cursor pagination to the feed in `feed/listing.py`. Write a new function `list_page(items, limit, cursor=None)`. It returns a dict with 2 keys: `"items"`, a list of at most `limit` items in the order of `list_items`, and `"next_cursor"`, a string or `None`. The first call has `cursor=None`, and a call with the `next_cursor` of a page returns the page after it. `next_cursor` is `None` when no item is left after the page, `limit` is a positive integer, and the caller never reads a cursor. Keep `list_items` as it is, and keep the tests in `tests/` as they are.
