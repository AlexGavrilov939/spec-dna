Extend `orders_of_day(store, day, zone="UTC")` in `orders/report.py`. `day` is a `datetime.date`, and `zone` is the name of an IANA time zone, for example `"America/New_York"`. The function returns the ids of the orders that were placed on that calendar day in that zone, sorted by `placed_at` and then by `id`. Without a zone it keeps its behavior of today: the day in UTC. Keep the tests in `tests/` as they are, and make them pass.
