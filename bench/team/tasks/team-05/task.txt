Add a class `SlugStore` in `slugs/store.py`. `SlugStore().add(name)` returns the slug of the name and keeps it in the store. When the slug is already in the store, `add` adds a suffix to it: `-2`, then `-3`, and so on, with the smallest number that gives a slug that is not in the store. Use `slugify` from `slugs/slug.py` to make the slug. Keep the tests in `tests/` as they are, and make them pass.
